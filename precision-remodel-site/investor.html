Scope of This Walkthrough — Be Clear on What You're Getting

This is a visual, non-invasive walkthrough. I assess what's visible and accessible — I don't open walls, pull up flooring, or test systems under load. Think of it as a highly informed set of contractor eyes on the property. That covers the majority of what drives rehab cost in a typical distressed property.

When something warrants deeper investigation — a crawl space that looks compromised, a panel that needs an electrician's evaluation, a foundation crack that may need an engineer's opinion — I'll tell you exactly what I saw and what the right next step is. Any follow-up diagnostic work is a separate engagement at a separate cost. I call it when the evidence warrants it, not to generate additional revenue. You're paying me for a straight answer — that's what you'll get.

The Underwriting Advantage

How This Changes
Your Deal Math

Most investors underwrite from the top down — ARV, then a percentage for rehab, then a gut-check. That approach consistently undercounts the hard stuff: the plumbing stack that needs replacing, the electrical panel that can't support the renovation, the foundation drainage that will come up on the buyer's evaluation after you've already finished the flip.

This walkthrough inverts the process. You start with what the building actually needs — not what you hope it needs — and build your offer from that number. A $99 visit that moves your rehab estimate from $45,000 to $62,000 before you close isn't a cost. It's the most valuable $99 in the deal.

You also get same-day or next-day scheduling when the deal requires it, and a written summary within 24 hours — because in this market, you're not waiting a week.

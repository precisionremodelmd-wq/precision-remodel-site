Why This Exists

Most Problems Don't Announce Themselves.

They build quietly behind walls, under floors, and above ceilings — until the moment they become emergencies that force your hand. A failing water heater gives you some warning. Active wood rot in a floor joist doesn't. Neither does a roof valley that's been wicking water into the sheathing for three seasons.

Most homeowners go years without having a licensed contractor walk through their property and tell them honestly what they're looking at. Not a salesperson, not a handyman, not a neighbor's recommendation — a licensed professional who can identify the problem, estimate the cost, and tell you when it needs to happen.

That's what this visit is. One flat fee. No estimate for work you didn't ask about. No pressure to sign anything. A straight read on your home, from someone who works on homes every day.

What This Walkthrough Includes — and What It Doesn't

This is a visual, non-invasive property walkthrough. I assess everything that's accessible and visible — roof condition, exposed framing, mechanical systems, surfaces, grading, drainage, and more — without opening walls, pulling up floors, or disassembling components. This is how professional property evaluations are conducted industry-wide, and it's thorough enough to identify the overwhelming majority of concerns that matter to a homeowner.

In some cases, what I observe will point toward something that warrants a closer look — moisture staining that may indicate active intrusion behind a surface, settlement patterns that suggest a structural engineer's review, or an electrical panel that needs a licensed electrician's hands on it. When that's the case, I'll tell you exactly what I saw, what I think it means, and what a proper follow-up evaluation would involve — at a separate cost. I won't recommend additional services unless the evidence in front of me genuinely warrants it. My value to you depends on you trusting what I tell you, and that starts with honesty on day one.

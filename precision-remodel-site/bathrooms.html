Why Precision Remodel

A Bathroom Should Earn
Its Square Footage.

Most bathroom remodels in Baltimore County follow a predictable pattern: fiberglass insert, builder-grade vanity, basic tile from a big-box store. It photographs poorly and feels it. Our clients have usually lived through at least one of those projects before calling us.

We approach bathrooms with the same attention we give kitchens — waterproofing done right, tile work planned before material is ordered, fixtures specified to the fixture rather than the category. Heated floors are asked for constantly; we install them in most primary bath projects because the cost is reasonable at this price point and the experience isn't.

Our minimum is $16,000 because below that number, there isn't enough scope to do the job correctly. We won't tell you otherwise to get the project.

Where We Work

Northeast Baltimore County.
That's It.

We serve a defined territory in NE Baltimore County and have chosen not to expand beyond it. The communities we work in are ones we know well — the housing stock, the permit offices, the neighborhoods. Focused geography means better execution.

Why We Stay Local

Better Work Happens
Close to Home.

Contractors who serve a 50-mile radius are optimizing for lead volume, not project quality. When you take jobs across multiple counties, your crew spends hours in transit, your supplier relationships spread thin, and your project manager is managing jobs they can't visit daily.

We work in NE Baltimore County because that's where Jonathan lives, where he knows the building inspectors, where he has long-standing relationships with local suppliers, and where he can be on your jobsite within 20 minutes if something needs attention.

It means we turn away projects outside this territory. We'd rather do fewer projects well than more projects adequately.

What We Know About These Neighborhoods

Built for NE Baltimore County Homes

Essex & Middle River

Much of the housing stock in Essex and Middle River dates from the 1950s through 1970s — ranchers, split-levels, and Cape Cods with narrow original kitchens and bathrooms that haven't been touched since the Clinton administration. These homes often have knob-and-tube or early Romex electrical that needs addressing before new kitchen work is done correctly. We know what to expect when we open these walls, and we price for it honestly. Investment in these neighborhoods typically runs $28,000–$55,000 for kitchens and $18,000–$32,000 for bathrooms depending on age of infrastructure.

White Marsh & Perry Hall

White Marsh and Perry Hall represent some of the most active remodeling markets in NE Baltimore County — colonials and townhomes from the 1980s and 1990s, often on their second or third owner, that have reached the point where original finishes are due for replacement. Kitchens are typically galley or L-shaped with good bones. The most common request: open the kitchen to the dining room or living room by removing a partial or full wall. These are achievable projects in the $38,000–$72,000 range and represent a large portion of our work.

Nottingham & Parkville

Nottingham and Parkville offer solid mid-century housing stock — many homes in the 21234 and 21236 zip codes haven't seen a real kitchen renovation since they were built. These are often the projects where clients are surprised by how much can be done at $35,000–$50,000: a full gut of the kitchen, new cabinetry, countertops, a tub-to-shower conversion in the main bath, and new flooring throughout. The scope is achievable because the footprints are manageable and the infrastructure, once opened, is usually in reasonable shape.

Towson, Lutherville & Timonium

Towson and the Lutherville-Timonium corridor include a wider range of housing — older stone and brick colonials in Towson proper, newer construction in Timonium, and everything in between. Project complexity and investment range reflect that variety. We've done $41,000 kitchen refreshes in Timonium townhomes and $90,000+ full renovations in Towson colonials. Primary suite bath renovations in this area often include heated floors, walk-in showers, and custom vanity cabinetry — the projects that use our full range of subcontractor coordination.

Cockeysville & Hunt Valley

The I-83 corridor north of Towson — Cockeysville through Hunt Valley — includes some of the largest homes in our service territory. These projects frequently involve full-suite primary bathroom expansions, high-end kitchen renovations with integrated appliances and custom cabinetry, and occasionally simultaneous multi-room scopes. Investment levels in these communities typically start at $45,000 for kitchens and can exceed $100,000 for full transformations. We're selective about project volume here to ensure we're not overcommitting our crew capacity.

Areas We Don't Serve

Outside Our Territory

We don't take projects in the following areas, regardless of project size:

  • — Baltimore City (all zip codes)
  • — Anne Arundel County (including Annapolis, Severna Park, Glen Burnie)
  • — Howard County
  • — Carroll County
  • — Harford County

If you're outside our service territory and looking for a quality remodeler, we're happy to point you toward the right resources, even if we can't take the project ourselves. Call us at 443-761-9209 and we'll do our best to help you find the right contractor for your area.

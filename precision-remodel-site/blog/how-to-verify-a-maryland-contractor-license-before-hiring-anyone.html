How to verify a Maryland contractor license before hiring anyone

July 21, 2026  ·  4 min read

Whether you're updating an aging kitchen in Perry Hall or giving a dated bathroom a fresh look in White Marsh, home remodeling is one of the most rewarding investments you can make in your property. But walking into a renovation project without the right knowledge can quickly turn an exciting upgrade into a stressful, over-budget ordeal. Understanding the fundamentals of home remodeling before breaking ground makes all the difference — and that's exactly what this guide is here to help you do.

Start With a Clear Plan and Realistic Budget

The foundation of any successful home remodeling project is thorough planning. Before you pick out cabinet finishes or tile patterns, sit down and define the scope of what you want to accomplish. Are you looking to improve functionality, increase resale value, or simply modernize an outdated space? The answer will shape every decision that follows.

Once your goals are clear, build a realistic budget that accounts for materials, labor, permits, and an often-overlooked contingency fund. Most experienced contractors recommend setting aside 10 to 15 percent of your total budget for unexpected costs. Homes in northeast Baltimore County — especially older builds common throughout communities like Essex and Parkville — frequently reveal hidden issues once walls or floors are opened up. Outdated plumbing, aging electrical systems, and water damage behind tile are common surprises that a solid contingency fund will help you handle without derailing the entire project.

Choose the Right Contractor for Your Project

Hiring the right contractor is arguably the most critical decision in your entire home remodeling process. Maryland requires general contractors to be licensed through the Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC), so always verify a contractor's license before signing anything. A licensed contractor carries proper insurance, pulls the necessary permits, and is accountable to state regulations — all of which protect you as a homeowner.

When interviewing contractors, ask for references from recent local projects, review their portfolio, and make sure they have documented experience with the specific type of work you need. A contractor who specializes in kitchen and bathroom remodeling will bring a level of detail and trade knowledge that a general handyman simply cannot match. Don't be afraid to ask questions about subcontractors, project timelines, and how they handle unexpected complications. The right contractor will welcome those conversations.

Understand the Permit Process in Baltimore County

Many homeowners are surprised to learn that even cosmetic-seeming renovations often require permits from Baltimore County. Structural changes, electrical upgrades, plumbing modifications, and certain additions all require proper permitting before work begins. Skipping this step isn't just a legal risk — it can create serious complications when you sell your home, as unpermitted work can delay or kill a sale entirely.

Your contractor should handle the permit process on your behalf and be familiar with the specific requirements for Baltimore County projects. The review and approval timeline can vary, so it's important to factor permitting into your overall project schedule. If you're planning a home remodeling project in communities like Towson or Cockeysville, where older neighborhoods often come with more complex permitting histories, working with a contractor who knows the local process is especially valuable.

Focus on High-ROI Spaces First

If you're remodeling with resale value in mind — or simply want the biggest visual and functional impact from your investment — kitchens and bathrooms consistently deliver the highest return on investment in home remodeling. These are the spaces buyers scrutinize most closely, and they're also the rooms where outdated finishes and worn materials are most immediately noticeable.

In the kitchen, upgrades like new cabinetry, quartz or granite countertops, modern lighting, and energy-efficient appliances can dramatically transform both the look and usability of the space. In bathrooms, walk-in showers with frameless glass enclosures, double vanities, and updated tile work are among the most popular upgrades for northeast Baltimore County homeowners

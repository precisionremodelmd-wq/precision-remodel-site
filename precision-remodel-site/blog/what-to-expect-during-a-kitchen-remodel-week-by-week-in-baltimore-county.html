What to expect during a kitchen remodel — week by week in Baltimore County

August 4, 2026  ·  2 min read

Starting a home remodel is one of the most exciting — and most significant — investments you can make as a homeowner. Whether you're dreaming of a chef-worthy kitchen in Perry Hall or a spa-inspired master bath in White Marsh, the path from idea to finished project involves more steps than most people anticipate. Understanding what to expect before the first hammer swings can mean the difference between a smooth, satisfying experience and a stressful one. Here's what northeast Baltimore County homeowners should know before diving into a remodeling project.

Understanding the Permit and Licensing Process in Maryland

Home remodeling in Maryland is a regulated industry, and for good reason. Work performed without proper permits can create safety hazards, complicate home sales, and even require expensive corrections down the road. In Baltimore County, most structural work, electrical upgrades, and plumbing changes require permits through the county's Department of Permits, Approvals, and Inspections.

When hiring a contractor, always verify their Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) license. This license is required by Maryland law for any contractor performing home improvement work valued at $500 or more. It provides homeowners with important legal protections, including access to the Maryland Home Improvement Guaranty Fund if a licensed contractor fails to complete work or causes damage.

Reputable contractors handle the permit process on your behalf. If a contractor suggests skipping permits to save time or money, that's a significant red flag — and a sign to walk away.
